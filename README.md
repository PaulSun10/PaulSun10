# Paul Sun

I'm a 16-year-old student from Hong Kong. I'm interested in computer science, software development, UI/UX design, and photography. My main project is [CubeFlow](https://cubeflow.com.cn), a native speedcubing app written in Swift and built with SwiftUI. I also develop and maintain its website and backend using Astro, TypeScript, and Go. I enjoy building new features, improving performance, and experimenting with different UI designs.

Speedcubing is one of my biggest hobbies. I represent Hong Kong, China and have competed in 19 WCA competitions across mainland China, Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, and Malaysia, including the Japan Championship 2026. I've competed in 13 different events, completed 540 official solves, and currently have 21 national top-100 rankings (12 singles and 9 averages). I mainly focus on 3×3 and Square-1, but I also enjoy solving big cubes and trying out different puzzles. My official 3×3 personal bests are 7.34 seconds for single and 9.28 seconds for average. Square-1 is my highest-ranked event, with an 8.62 single (NR 15) and a 12.42 average (NR 16). I'm currently learning FTO (Face-Turning Octahedron) as well.

I also spend a lot of time playing sports. I play varsity volleyball as an outside hitter and varsity basketball as a shooting guard. I enjoy playing badminton and soccer in my free time, as well as taking photos at school events, sports games, and cubing competitions.

Learning languages is another hobby of mine. I speak Cantonese, Mandarin, English, and Japanese. I've passed the JLPT N2 and am currently preparing for N1. I'm also learning Korean in my free time.

## Featured Project

### CubeFlow

An all-in-one speedcubing app built with Swift and SwiftUI.

- Advanced timer with multiple input methods
- Detailed solve analytics and session management
- Smart Cube integration with real-time tracking and scramble guidance
- Extensive algorithm library and training tools
- Interactive virtual cubes
- WCA profiles, results, and competition discovery
- Live competition results, reports, and result sharing
- Local and mixed-input Battle
- Extensive customization and support for 20 languages

**Currently available in public beta on TestFlight.**

[Website](https://cubeflow.com.cn) · [Repository](https://github.com/PaulSun10/CubeFlow) · [Releases](https://github.com/PaulSun10/CubeFlow/releases) · [Download on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/jmAchhpp)
